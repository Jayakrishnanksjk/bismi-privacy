Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026
This Privacy Policy explains how Bismi Hypermarket (“Bismi”, “we”, “our” or “us”) collects, uses, discloses and protects your personal and device information when you use our Bismi mobile application, our website and related services (collectively, the “Platform”). Please read it carefully. By accessing or using the Platform you consent to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the Platform.
Summary: We collect only what we need to run the app — your account details, order and delivery information, device and usage data, and (with your permission) location and notification tokens. We use it to fulfil orders, run the app, prevent fraud and, with your consent, send offers. We do not sell your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information you provide

  • Account details: full name, email address, phone number, date of birth, gender, profile photo, username and password.
  • Delivery details: delivery addresses, delivery instructions and contact details for the recipient.
  • Transaction details: order contents, order value, payment method and status, delivery preferences, invoices, refunds and purchase history.
  • Communications: messages, reviews, ratings, support requests and feedback you send us, including through customer care.
  • Verification details: one-time passwords (OTP) and information you provide to verify your account or phone number.

1.2 Information collected automatically (device and usage data)

  • Device identifiers, device model, operating system and app version.
  • IP address, network type and approximate location derived from the IP address.
  • App usage data such as screens viewed, features used, session time, crash logs and diagnostic data.
  • Persistent identifiers such as advertising / push notification tokens (where available).
  • Cookie and similar tracker identifiers when you access the Platform through a browser.

1.3 Location information

With your consent, we collect precise or approximate location to show stores and products available near you, verify delivery areas, and calculate delivery availability. You can turn location off at any time in your device settings; some delivery features may then be unavailable.

1.4 Notifications

If you enable notifications, we collect your device’s push notification token so we can send you order updates, delivery alerts and (where permitted) promotional messages. You can disable notifications in app or device settings at any time.

1.5 Photos and files

If you choose to upload a profile picture or attach images (for example to a support request), we access only the image you select through the device picker — we do not read your full photo library or other files.

1.6 Information from third parties

We may receive information from payment partners (payment status), delivery partners (delivery status), analytics providers, and from account providers when you choose to sign in with them.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create, secure and manage your account.
  • To process, fulfil, deliver and track your orders and handle returns, refunds and cancellations.
  • To show store availability, offers and products relevant to your location and past orders.
  • To send order, delivery and service messages, and — with your consent — promotional offers.
  • To provide customer support and respond to your requests.
  • To detect, prevent and investigate fraud, abuse and security incidents.
  • To understand usage trends, debug, improve and optimise the Platform.
  • To comply with legal, tax, accounting and regulatory obligations (including GST requirements in India).

5. Data Retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described in this Policy — for example, for the life of your account and afterwards for the period required by tax, accounting and consumer-protection law (including GST record-keeping requirements in India). When data is no longer needed, we securely delete or anonymise it. Aggregated, anonymised data that cannot identify you may be kept indefinitely.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS), secure servers, access controls, permission-based internal access and periodic security reviews. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please keep your account credentials confidential and contact us immediately if you suspect unauthorised use of your account.

7. Your Rights and Choices

Subject to applicable law, you may request to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data (you can edit most details directly in the app).
  • Delete your account and personal data, subject to legal retention duties.
  • Restrict or object to certain processing.
  • Port your data in a portable format.
  • Withdraw consent at any time (for example, location, notifications or marketing messages).
  • Opt out of marketing by using the unsubscribe option in messages or by contacting us.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 11. We respond within a reasonable time and no later than the period required by applicable law.

8. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website and app may use cookies, local storage, SDKs and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember preferences, measure performance and understand usage. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Third-party analytics providers may set their own cookies or identifiers on our behalf; they process data under their own privacy policies and our instructions.

9. Children’s Privacy

The Platform is intended for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we learn that a child has provided us with personal data, we will take steps to delete it promptly. If you believe a child has provided us with data, please contact us.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top shows when it was most recently revised. Material changes will be highlighted in the app or by other notice. Continued use of the Platform after changes take effect means you accept the revised Policy.
